## One-paragraph summary
ChatGPT's release on 30 November 2022 turned the pre-assembled ingredients of the previous era (GPT-3.5 + RLHF) into a mass consumer product that reached 100 million monthly users within two months, and the four years since have been a compressed arms race across three fronts [1][2]. On the *model* front, frontier labs shipped multimodal generalists — GPT-4 (14 Mar 2023), Claude (Mar 2023), Gemini (6 Dec 2023), GPT-4o (May 2024) — while diffusion models (DALL-E 2, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion in 2022; Sora video in 2024) made image, audio and video generation ordinary [3][4][5][6][7][8]. On the *openness* front, Meta's LLaMA weights leaked in March 2023 and Llama 2/3 were then released deliberately; Mistral, Qwen and DeepSeek followed, and DeepSeek-R1 (20 Jan 2025) showed an open-weights Chinese lab matching OpenAI's o1 reasoning model at a fraction of the reported cost [9][10][11][12][13]. On the *agency* front, "test-time compute" reasoning models (o1, Sep 2024), computer-use (Oct 2024), the Model Context Protocol (Nov 2024) and terminal coding agents (Claude Code, 2025) turned chat assistants into systems that plan, call tools and act — reviving symbolic-era questions about planning and world models inside a connectionist substrate [14][15][16][17]. Around all of this grew an unprecedented governance and hardware superstructure: the Bletchley Declaration (Nov 2023), the EU AI Act (in force 1 Aug 2024), Nvidia's Hopper→Blackwell cycle and multi-trillion-dollar valuation, and the 2024 Nobel Prizes in Physics (Hopfield, Hinton) and Chemistry (Baker, Hassabis, Jumper) that formally canonised deep learning [18][19][20][21][22][23].

## Schools of thought / sub-clusters
- **Closed frontier labs (scaling + RLHF)** — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind: proprietary weights, API distribution, safety cases and staged deployment; borders: Transformer Plateau to the west (GPT-3, InstructGPT), alignment plateau and policy coast to the north. [1][3][4][5]
- **Open-weights movement** — Meta (Llama), Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, Stability, EleutherAI/Hugging Face commons, llama.cpp/Ollama local-inference tooling; borders: the open-vs-closed road (file 09e) and the earlier open-source frameworks district (TensorFlow/PyTorch); the OSI's 2024 Open Source AI Definition contests whether "open weights" is truly open; by 2026 the open frontier is largely Chinese (Qwen, DeepSeek, Kimi) plus Google's Gemma, after Meta's retreat to the closed Muse line. [9][12][13][30][74][116][118][120]
- **Diffusion / generative-media district** — DALL-E 2, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion/SDXL/SD3, Sora/Sora 2, Veo 3, Genie world models; audio via ElevenLabs, Suno, Udio; conditioning inherited via the CLIP bridge from file 07; borders: computer-vision lineage (file 09g), copyright litigation (NYT, Getty, RIAA suits) and deepfake ethics (file 09c). [6][7][8][24][25][87][92][108][112]
- **Reasoning & test-time-compute school** — o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1, Gemini "thinking" models: RL on verifiable rewards, long chains of thought, inference-time scaling as a new axis; borders: the reinforcement-learning river (file 09a) and game-playing highlands (AlphaZero/MuZero search-plus-learning). [11][14][59]
- **Agents & tooling coast** — ReAct, Toolformer, LangChain/AutoGPT scaffolds, function calling, RAG, MCP/A2A, computer use, Operator, Devin/Cursor/Claude Code/Cowork coding and desktop agents, SWE-bench/GAIA/Arena evals; the most "mixed" Y-position of the era because it re-imports symbolic planning, tool APIs and protocols; borders: symbolic/planning eras (STRIPS, Shakey) far to the west via a long "road", and robotics/VLA (RT-2, Gemini Robotics, π0, Helix) to the embodied shore. [15][16][17][39][58][70][71][77][84][89][95][96][114]
- **Alignment & interpretability plateau** — Constitutional AI, DPO, RLHF refinements, Responsible Scaling Policy / lab safety frameworks, mechanistic interpretability, evals and red-teaming; borders: AI safety river (file 09c), Bletchley/EU AI Act policy coast, safety institutes (UK AISI). [26][37][75][88][107]
- **Policy & governance coast** — Bletchley (2023), EO 14110 (2023, rescinded by EO 14179 in 2025), EU AI Act (2024), safety institutes, the Paris Action Summit (2025), the copyright docket (NYT v. OpenAI, Getty v. Stability); borders: ethics tributaries from Weizenbaum→Stochastic Parrots and existential-risk tributaries from Bostrom/FLI/CAIS. [18][19][33][34][42][105][106][92]
- **Compute & hardware hinterland** — Nvidia Hopper/Blackwell, TPU generations, inference challengers (Groq LPU), US export controls (Oct 2022 → Jan 2025 diffusion rule), Stargate-scale data-centre build-out; borders: hardware river (file 09d), the Chinese Shore (DeepSeek's efficiency response to export controls). [21][22][47][76][103][104]
