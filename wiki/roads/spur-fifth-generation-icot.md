## Historical & Architectural Rationale

Traces the logic-programming national computing initiatives from Colmerauer & Roussel's Prolog (1972) to Japan's MITI launching the Fifth Generation Computer Systems project at ICOT built on concurrent Prolog (1982), the US DARPA Strategic Computing Initiative & UK Alvey response (1983), and the conclusion of ICOT (1992).
