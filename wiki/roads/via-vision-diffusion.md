# The ConvNet, GAN & Open Diffusion Media Coast Road

**Road ID**: `via-vision-diffusion` · **Hierarchy Class**: `trunk` · **Stops**: `16` · [Back to Roads Index](/roads/index.md)

## Historical & Architectural Rationale

Traces the academic and open-weights computer vision and latent diffusion spine from ImageNet (2009), ILSVRC (2010), and AlexNet (2012) through R-CNN (2013), MS COCO (2014), VGG/GoogLeNet (2014), BatchNorm (2015), U-Net/YOLO (2015), ResNet (2015), pix2pix/CycleGAN (2017), DDPM (2020), Dhariwal & Nichol's Diffusion Beats GANs (2021), Rombach et al.'s Stable Diffusion (2022), ControlNet (2023), SDXL (2023), to SD3 & Black Forest Labs Flux.1 (2024).

## 3-Test Knowledge Graph Audit

- **Lawyer Test**: Eliminates the old cross-company media chain that routed OpenAI `sora-2024` into Google `imagen3-2024 -> notebooklm-audio-2024 -> veo3-2025` and back into OpenAI `sora2-2025`.
- **Reddit Test**: Keeps the open academic ConvNet -> U-Net -> DDPM -> Latent Diffusion (Stable Diffusion -> ControlNet -> SDXL -> SD3/Flux.1) spine pure, while OpenAI multimodal (`branch-openai-media`) and Google multimodal (`branch-google-media`) have their own dedicated branches.
- **Individuals Involved Test**: Robin Rombach, Björn Ommer, Lvmin Zhang, and the academic computer vision community have a clean, accurate lineage.
