## Historical & Architectural Rationale

Traces the philosophical and embodied robotics critique of symbolic AI from Joseph Weizenbaum's ELIZA (1966) and Computer Power and Human Reason (1976) to John Searle's Chinese Room argument (1980), Hubert & Stuart Dreyfus's Mind over Machine (1986), Winograd & Flores's Understanding Computers and Cognition (1986), Hans Moravec's Paradox (1988), and Rodney Brooks's 'Elephants Don't Play Chess' subsumption architecture (1990).
