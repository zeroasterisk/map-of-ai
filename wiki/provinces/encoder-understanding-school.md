## Milestones in This Province

- `2018.05` — [ULMFiT](/milestones/ulmfit-2018.md): Howard & Ruder: LM pre-training + fine-tuning transfers to text classification (18 Jan)
- `2018.12` — [ELMo](/milestones/elmo-2018.md): Peters et al.: contextual embeddings from a bidirectional LSTM — the last pre-Transformer win
- `2018.3` — [GLUE](/milestones/glue-2018.md): Wang, Bowman et al. (NYU/UW/DeepMind): the leaderboard BERT, XLNet, RoBERTa, T5 race up
- `2018.78` — [BERT](/milestones/bert-2018.md): Devlin et al. (11 Oct 2018): encoder-only bidirectional pre-training tops GLUE, SQuAD, SWAG
- `2019.02` — [Transformer-XL](/milestones/transformer-xl-2019.md): CMU/Google (9 Jan 2019): recurrence over segments for longer context
- `2019.34` — [SuperGLUE](/milestones/superglue-2019.md): a stickier benchmark (2 May 2019) after models pass GLUE's human baseline
- `2019.47` — [XLNet](/milestones/xlnet-2019.md): generalised autoregressive pre-training (19 Jun 2019) beats BERT on 20 tasks
- `2019.57` — [RoBERTa](/milestones/roberta-2019.md): Facebook AI (26 Jul 2019): BERT trained longer on more data retakes GLUE
- `2019.75` — [DistilBERT](/milestones/distilbert-2019.md): Hugging Face (2 Oct 2019): distillation for deployment — 40 % smaller, 60 % faster
- `2019.81` — [T5 · C4](/milestones/t5-2019.md): Raffel et al. (23 Oct 2019): every NLP task as text-to-text; introduces the C4 corpus
- `2020.4` — [RAG](/milestones/rag-2020.md): Lewis et al.: retrieval-augmented generation — the eastern border of the encoder camp
- `2020.45` — [DeBERTa](/milestones/deberta-2020.md): He et al. (Microsoft, Jun 2020): disentangled attention surpasses human baseline on SuperGLUE
