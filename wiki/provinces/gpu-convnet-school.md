## Milestones in This Province

- `2009.5` — [ImageNet](/milestones/imagenet-2009.md): Deng et al., CVPR 2009: ~14 M images labelled via Mechanical Turk
- `2009.9` — [GPU deep nets](/milestones/gpu-dbn-2009.md): Raina, Madhavan & Ng: GPUs train a 100 M-parameter DBN up to 70× faster
- `2010.5` — [ILSVRC begins](/milestones/ilsvrc-2010.md): first ImageNet challenge: 1,000 classes; 2010–11 winners are SVM pipelines
- `2011.5` — [IDSIA DanNet](/milestones/dannet-2011.md): Cireşan et al.: first superhuman result in a visual contest (IJCNN traffic signs)
- `2012.5` — [Dropout](/milestones/dropout-hinton-2012.md): Hinton et al. introduce Dropout regularization to prevent co-adaptation of feature detectors
- `2012.75` — [AlexNet](/milestones/alexnet-2012.md): Krizhevsky, Sutskever & Hinton win ILSVRC 2012: 15.3 % top-5 error vs 26.2 %
- `2013.85` — [R-CNN](/milestones/rcnn-2013.md): Girshick, Donahue, Darrell & Malik (Berkeley): CNN features for object detection
- `2014.35` — [MS COCO](/milestones/coco-2014.md): Lin et al.: the detection / segmentation benchmark after ImageNet
- `2014.7` — [VGG · GoogLeNet](/milestones/vgg-googlenet-2014.md): 16–22-layer nets (Oxford, Google); GoogLeNet wins ILSVRC 2014
- `2015.15` — [Batch Normalization](/milestones/batchnorm-2015.md): Ioffe and Szegedy introduce BatchNorm, stabilizing and accelerating deep network training
- `2015.4` — [U-Net · YOLO](/milestones/unet-yolo-2015.md): Ronneberger et al. (Freiburg) segmentation; Redmon et al. real-time detection
- `2015.9` — [ResNet](/milestones/resnet-2015.md): He, Zhang, Ren & Sun (MSRA): 152 layers, 3.57 % top-5, wins ILSVRC 2015
