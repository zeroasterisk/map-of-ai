## Milestones in This Province

- `1666.5` — [Calculus Ratiocinator](/milestones/leibniz-calculus-1666.md): Leibniz proposes a universal formal language and mechanical calculus of human reasoning
- `1804.5` — [Jacquard Loom](/milestones/jacquard-loom-1804.md): Joseph Marie Jacquard's punched-card loom inspires Babbage's programmable store and mill
- `1843.5` — [Analytical Engine](/milestones/babbage-lovelace-1843.md): Babbage designs the Analytical Engine; Ada Lovelace publishes the first algorithm and objection
- `1854.5` — [Laws of Thought](/milestones/boole-laws-of-thought-1854.md): George Boole formalizes propositional logic as binary algebraic symbol manipulation
- `1879.5` — [Begriffsschrift](/milestones/frege-begriffsschrift-1879.md): Gottlob Frege invents modern first-order predicate calculus with universal quantifiers
- `1910.5` — [Principia Mathematica](/milestones/principia-mathematica-1910.md): Whitehead and Russell derive mathematics from formal symbolic axioms and rules
- `1928.5` — [Entscheidungsproblem](/milestones/hilbert-programme-1928.md): Hilbert and Ackermann pose the Entscheidungsproblem: can an algorithm decide formal validity?
- `1931.2` — [Gödel Incompleteness](/milestones/godel-incompleteness-1931.md): Kurt Gödel proves arithmetic contains true statements unprovable within any formal system
