# Map of AI — Lineage Roads & Milestones Knowledge Bundle (OKF v0.2)

This Open Knowledge Format (OKF v0.2) bundle is the authoritative, machine- and human-verifiable Knowledge Graph (KG) governing all **76 lineage roads** (`10` Continental Trunks, `14` Cross-Province Branches, and `52` Thematic & Lab Spurs), **47 provinces**, and **475 historical AI milestones** (1666–2026) in *Map of AI — The 2036 Textbook Atlas*.

Every road and directed edge (`upstream ← West` to `downstream → East`) has been audited against three explicit gates:

1. **Lawyer Test**: Zero false cross-competitor proprietary IP derivation claims, zero conflated corporate titles (e.g., `gpt4-2023` is unbundled from Claude 1 and `microsoft-openai-2023` is unbundled from Google Bard), and strict separation between federal copyright lawsuits and statutory safety legislation.
2. **Reddit Test**: Zero nearest-neighbor spatial accidents (such as `gpt4o-2024 -> nobel-2024`), zero multi-lab zig-zags across competing open-weights or media models, and `0` chronological reversals across all `475` milestones.
3. **Individuals Involved Test**: Researchers, laureates, and founders (Demis Hassabis, John Jumper, Geoffrey Hinton, John Hopfield, Rich Sutton, Andrew Barto, Yann LeCun, Dario Amodei, Timnit Gebru, Ilya Sutskever, Tri Dao, and others) are connected strictly along their own published papers, institutions, and awards.
